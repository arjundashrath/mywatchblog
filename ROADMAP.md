# The Wrist Journal Growth & Monetization Roadmap

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This checklist is the project source of truth. Update task statuses as work is completed and link relevant PRs or reports. Unchecked tasks are planned work, not confirmed defects.

## Goals

- [ ] Reach 1,000 monthly organic search sessions across search engines.
- [ ] Reduce Reddit's share of sessions below 50% by growing other channels while retaining Reddit readership.
- [ ] Build consistent traffic to articles without needing to promote every article on Reddit.

Measure goals using complete calendar months. Reddit share will remain an estimate until attribution improves.

## Current baseline

As discussed on 10 October 2026:

- The site receives little meaningful traffic when articles are not promoted on Reddit.
- Amazon Associates is the only affiliate program currently used.
- GA4 reporting for 1 February to 9 October 2026: 5,185 active users and 6,218 sessions.
- Session sources include Direct / none (4,300), Reddit referral (686), ChatGPT (389), Google (349), Bing (232) and other sources (262).
- The owner believes most Direct traffic comes from Reddit. This is an observation, not independently verified attribution. Do not treat all Direct sessions as confirmed Reddit traffic.
- These traffic totals cover the stated reporting period and are not monthly baselines.

## 1. Affiliate monetization: immediate priority

- [ ] Register with Cuelinks and obtain approval for the site.
- [ ] Verify active Flipkart and other relevant retailer campaigns inside the account, including watch eligibility, actual commission rates, attribution windows, app tracking and payment terms.
- [ ] Retain direct Amazon Associates links and ensure any Cuelinks automation does not overwrite them unintentionally.
- [ ] Audit existing retailer links across buying guides, reviews and sale articles.
- [ ] Convert eligible Flipkart and other retailer links to affiliate links, then verify destinations and tracking.
- [ ] Check affiliate disclosures and link attributes across articles.
- [ ] Keep recommending the best suitable retailer and deal regardless of commission.
- [ ] Review validated commissions after the first complete reporting cycle before expanding integration.

## 2. Analytics and attribution: immediate priority

- [ ] Use consistent UTM tags for new Reddit promotion links, with source=reddit, medium=social and an article-specific campaign.
- [ ] Verify that tagged visits appear under the intended source and medium in GA4.
- [ ] Track outbound retailer clicks with article path, retailer, watch/model and link position where practical.
- [ ] Verify outbound click events on desktop and mobile without collecting personal information.
- [ ] Use article-specific affiliate tracking IDs or sub-IDs where each program supports them.
- [ ] Review engagement and landing pages for Reddit, search and AI referrals, including ChatGPT.
- [ ] Create a monthly scorecard covering sessions by channel, organic sessions, leading landing pages, retailer clicks. Keep sales and earnings reporting private.
- [ ] Keep retailer clicks separate from purchases; only report article-level conversions where affiliate reporting supports attribution.

## 3. SEO and organic growth

- [ ] Audit Google Search Console coverage, indexing, sitemap submission and search performance.
- [ ] Check Bing Webmaster Tools setup and indexing.
- [ ] Investigate priority articles that are excluded from indexing.
- [ ] Review titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and headings on existing evergreen guides.
- [ ] Check mobile usability, page speed and image loading on important landing pages.
- [ ] Validate structured data against the content actually present.
- [ ] Add useful internal links between budget guides, reviews and related educational articles.
- [ ] Map Indian watch search queries to existing articles and identify content gaps without creating overlapping guides unnecessarily.
- [ ] Refresh pricing, availability, broken links and outdated recommendations on priority articles.
- [ ] Review organic search progress monthly using impressions, clicks, queries and landing pages alongside GA4 sessions.

## 4. Content pipeline

- [ ] Build an editorial calendar balancing evergreen buying guides, hands-on reviews, educational articles and timely sale coverage.
- [ ] Prioritize existing guides with search impressions or strong retailer-click activity for improvements.
- [ ] Publish more original ownership observations and photographs where available.
- [ ] Keep Indian pricing, availability, wrist fit and practical tradeoffs central to recommendations.
- [ ] Maintain the established voice: natural paragraphs, light humor, no em dashes and no invented testing claims.
- [ ] Schedule regular checks for sale posts so expired deals are updated or clearly marked.
- [ ] Review which topics attract readers without Reddit promotion and use that evidence to guide future articles.

## 5. Traffic diversification

- [ ] Continue useful Reddit participation and article promotion within each community's rules.
- [ ] Grow Google and Bing discovery through the SEO and content work above.
- [ ] Investigate which pages attract AI referrals and whether those visitors engage or click retailer links.
- [ ] Evaluate a simple email subscription or other repeat-reader channel after the initial tracking and SEO work.
- [ ] Compare traffic on promoted and unpromoted articles to measure independent discovery.
- [ ] Reassess channel concentration monthly once Reddit attribution improves.

## 6. AdSense readiness

- [ ] Review site navigation, About, contact, privacy and affiliate disclosure pages for completeness.
- [ ] Audit thin, duplicated or outdated content and improve it with useful original material.
- [ ] Review overall site usability and content quality before another application.
- [ ] Reapply once identified issues are addressed, without assuming approval is guaranteed.

## Review routine

Review monthly. Record the reporting period, changes completed, results and next priorities. Check off a task only after it is verified, and include a PR or other evidence when available.
